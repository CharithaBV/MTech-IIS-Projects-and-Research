## Curriculum Structure

The curriculum (64 credits total) covers foundational theory, core technical subjects, electives, and dissertation research:

- **Foundation Core:** Advanced Mathematics (Linear Algebra & Optimization), Computer-Based Industrial Control, Advanced Control Systems, and Architecture of Intelligent Systems.
- **Subject Core:** Data Analytics & Data Mining, Embedded System Design, Computational Intelligence, Fault Diagnostic Systems, Modeling & Simulation Lab, Industrial Electronics Lab, and Research Methodology.
- **Electives & Applied Areas:** Industrial IoT, Cyber Security for Industrial Systems, Deep Learning, and Industrial Robotics.
- **Dissertation:** Two-phase research project (20IS798 & 20IS799, 20 credits) culminating in experimental evaluation and peer-reviewed publication.

## Research Publication

### Machine Learning Based Fault Detection in Induction Motor using Thermal Imaging

- **Authors:** Charitha B.V. and Dr. T. Ananthan
- **Venue:** 2022 3rd International Conference on Electronics and Sustainable Communication Systems (ICESC)
- **Publisher:** IEEE (Date: 17–19 August 2022)
- **DOI:** [`10.1109/ICESC54411.2022.9885282`](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICESC54411.2022.9885282) \| [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9885282/)

**Summary:**  
The paper addresses non-contact fault detection in three-phase induction motors using infrared thermography. Surface temperature patterns under normal and fault conditions (such as bearing wear, phase unbalance, and overheating) were captured using a thermal camera. Statistical features were extracted from the thermal images and used to train machine learning classifiers for automated fault diagnosis.
