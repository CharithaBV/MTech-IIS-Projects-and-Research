## Executive Summary & Program Scope

The **Master of Technology in Industrial Intelligent Systems (IIS)** at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is an interdisciplinary post-graduate specialization at the intersection of **Electrical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Edge Computing, and Cyber-Physical Systems**. 

The program focuses on transforming conventional industrial processes into self-monitoring, adaptive, and cognitive manufacturing environments. Key research pillars include:

1. **Intelligent Condition Monitoring & Diagnostics:** Early fault detection, acoustic/vibration/thermal signature analysis, and remaining useful life (RUL) estimation for critical electromechanical equipment.
2. **Industrial Computer Vision & Infrared Thermography:** Non-destructive testing (NDT), automated visual inspection, and thermal image analysis for process automation.
3. **Machine Learning & Deep Learning for Industry 4.0:** Supervised and unsupervised pattern recognition, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and reinforcement learning for control systems.
4. **Cyber-Physical Systems & Industrial IoT:** Sensor telemetry, embedded intelligence, distributed control systems, and edge analytics for smart manufacturing.

This repository serves as an open academic repository archiving research publications, experimental codebases, technical monographs, and presentation materials developed during the M.Tech program.

## Featured Publication

### **Machine Learning Based Fault Detection in Induction Motor using Thermal Imaging**

* **Authors:** **Charitha B.V.** and **T. Ananthan**
* **Conference:** *2022 3rd International Conference on Electronics and Sustainable Communication Systems (ICESC)*
* **Date of Conference:** 17–19 August 2022
* **Publisher:** IEEE
* **DOI:** [`10.1109/ICESC54411.2022.9885282`](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICESC54411.2022.9885282)
* **Access Link:** [IEEE Xplore Document 9885282](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9885282/)

#### Abstract & Research Contributions
> Three-phase induction motors represent the backbone of heavy industrial drives. Unplanned downtime resulting from mechanical or electrical faults causes severe operational and financial impacts. This research presents a non-invasive condition monitoring and automated fault diagnosis methodology leveraging **Infrared Thermography (IRT)** combined with **Machine Learning (ML)** algorithms. By capturing spatial temperature distributions across operating induction motors under baseline and faulted regimes (e.g., bearing degradation, stator winding faults, and phase imbalance), distinctive thermal feature signatures were extracted and classified with high statistical reliability, eliminating the need for intrusive sensor installations.
